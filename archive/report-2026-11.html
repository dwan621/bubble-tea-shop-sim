1.Background: why this problem项目背景：为什么要研究这个问题

A small bubble-tea shop serves four order classes from one cross-trained clerk pool. Two decisions are easy to state and hard to answer quantitatively: should the shop triage fast items (bread, ice cream) ahead of the slow tea queue, and should a mixed order (tea + ice cream) hand the ice cream over immediately ("split") instead of waiting for the tea ("synchro")?

The original course project answered these questions with an AnyLogic model and three single-purpose Python scripts. The AnyLogic runs cannot be repeated - only the screenshots survive - and the scripts each had their own seed convention and statistics window. This report keeps the questions and the answers, but rebuilds the evidence as one deterministic engine plus five experiments whose numbers can be regenerated from this repository in minutes.

课程原始项目用 AnyLogic 模型加三个一次性 Python 脚本回答了这两个问题。 AnyLogic 模型已无法重跑（只剩截图存档），三个脚本的随机种子与统计口径也各不相同。 本报告保留问题与结论，但把证据重建为一个确定性仿真引擎 + 五组实验：所有数字都能在 本仓库内用固定随机种子重新生成，结果以 JSON 形式提交。

What is new compared with the archived study

  • A fine policy grid. 356 cells: arrival rate 0.30-1.30 in steps of 0.05 × 1-5 clerks × the 2×2 policy matrix, 200 replicates each (60 in the near-overload cells), with paired differences computed on common random numbers.
  • A break-even load instead of a break-even interval. The original report could only say "between λ = 1.10 and 1.25". A bisection on the paired profit difference now returns λ* = 1.147 (ρ* = 80.9%) for three clerks, with an identification band from the confidence intervals.
  • An honest sensitivity analysis. Wage, waiting cost, order mix and tea make time are varied one at a time; the first two turn out to be exact linear transforms of a single simulation run, which the report verifies numerically rather than presenting as new experiments.
  • Three behaviour mechanisms. Balking, reneging and a dynamic ice-cream priority rule - none of them in the archived study.
  • A line-by-line audit of the archived numbers, including one archival row that cannot be reproduced from its own fields.

Two kinds of numbers in this report

Every table is tagged. Python reproducible means the number was produced just now by bubble_tea_sim through the scripts in analysis/, and is stored in results/*.json. AnyLogic archive means the number was read from the archived AnyLogic screenshots in docs/assets/slides/ and the course experiment log; the AnyLogic model itself is not available, so those numbers can only be compared, never regenerated.

2.What the model actually is模型口径

The shop is modelled as one queueing system. Customers arrive as a Poisson process with rate λ; each is a single order from one of four classes. A job is one uninterruptible block of clerk time: on a pure order it is order-taking plus making, on a mixed order it is either one long job (synchronised delivery) or two jobs (split delivery). All clerks are cross-trained and share one FIFO queue with a priority override for fast items, so the clerk pool is the only bottleneck.

模型把门店视为单一排队系统：泊松到达，四类订单，一个共享队列，员工全部跨岗。 "任务（job）"是不可中断的一段员工工时。纯订单只有一个任务（点单+制作），混合单在同步交付下 是一个长任务、在拆分交付下是两个任务。瓶颈只有员工池一处。

p̄ = 0.55(2.500) + 0.20(0.667) + 0.05(0.833) + 0.20(2.833) = 2.1167 clerk-minutes per customer.

The engine is a small event loop: a binary-heap event calendar, a priority/FIFO job list and a pool of clerk free-times. Arrivals are drawn for the whole horizon up front, so behaviour rules can never feed back into the arrival stream - which is what makes the common-random-number comparisons in this report valid. Given a seed the run is deterministic; two different seeds are independent replicates.

引擎是标准库实现的离散事件循环：二叉堆事件表、优先级+FIFO 任务表、员工空闲时间表。 到达流在开跑前一次性生成，因此行为规则不会反过来改变到达序列——这正是本报告"公共随机数" 配对比较成立的前提。给定随机种子，一次运行完全确定。

Statistics window

Each replicate simulates a 480-minute day with a 60-minute warm-up: customers who arrive during the first hour still receive service (and still occupy the clerks), but they are excluded from the reported means. This mirrors the archived study and keeps the queue in steady state at the start of the measurement window. Section 5 shows the price of this choice and how to undo it.

3.Two delivery strategies两种交付策略

Both strategies produce exactly the same total clerk work for a mixed order, so utilisation cannot be a decision criterion - the policies differ only in how the waiting is distributed between the mixed customer, the tea queue and the fast-item queue. That is the theoretical reason the split/synchro question is interesting, and it is also why the break-even in section 7 is driven by queueing nonlinearity rather than by capacity.

两种交付方式的总工作量完全相同，因此利用率不能作为决策指标； 差别只在于等待如何分配给不同顾客。这也解释了为什么第 7 节的盈亏平衡点由排队非线性决定， 而不是由产能决定。

Where the ice-cream priority applies

The ice-cream component of a mixed order can jump the queue; the tea component never does. This is the "validity correction" the original study had to make after its first mixed-order table was withdrawn: letting the whole mixed order jump would double-count the priority and break the workload identity.

The reported profit level is deliberately conservative

The engine counts revenue for arrivals after warm-up (87.5% of the day) but charges the full eight hours of wages. The measured profit is therefore an affine transform of the steady-state profit: profit_ss = (profit + wage) / 0.875 - wage. For λ = 1.25, m = 3 that gives 322.6 per hour instead of 272.9. Because the transform is affine with a positive slope it does not change any ranking, any break-even point or the location of the profit maximum - only the level.

The same correction reproduces the archival experiment almost exactly: the E-series runs used no warm-up, and their Python recomputation gives 323.3 per hour at λ = 1.25 versus 322.6 here. Two different measurement windows, one formula.

课程四讲给了可检验的预测：Little 定律、产能与利用率、多服务台等待近似、MPS/BOM/CRP。 本节先只用参数算出预测，再用 200 次重复的仿真去验证，偏差在 85% 利用率以下不超过 1%。 经济层需要注意一个口径：引擎只统计预热后到达的顾客（占全天 87.5%）的收入，却计入整整 8 小时的工资， 因此报告的利润水平是偏保守的仿射变换，不改变任何排序与盈亏平衡点。

Three caveats you should read before quoting a number

  • Profit levels are conservative (section 4): the warm-up hour is paid but not billed. Rankings are unaffected.
  • Overload cells are not decision-grade. Once ρ ≥ 1 the queue never empties within the horizon, so the mean LOS depends on where the run is stopped. Those cells are shown for completeness but excluded from every conclusion; the sweep runs them with 60 replicates only.
  • LOS under balking is conditional. Customers who walk away are not served and therefore not in the LOS average, so a balking rule mechanically lowers mean LOS. Section 9 therefore reports the abandonment rate next to every LOS number.

Reading the sweep correctly

  • Below ~60% utilisation the four policies are within about 4.9 per hour of each other: triage has nothing to triage.
  • Between 60% and 85% fast priority is a cheap win: it buys +12.7 per hour at λ = 1.25 while costing the tea queue only a few tenths of a minute.
  • Above the break-even load (section 7) split delivery becomes a net cost, because it inserts a short high-priority job in front of a long queue.
  • Above ρ ≈ 0.95 no policy helps: only capacity does.

策略扫描是整项研究的主干：356 个配置、共 65,040 个仿真"天"，全部使用公共随机数配对。 三个形状立刻可见：产能压倒一切（λ=1.25 时多一名员工价值 +51.2/小时）； 快品优先的收益随负载放大（λ=1.0 +1.90，1.25 时 +12.7， 1.30 时 +21.1）；拆分交付则是一个"随负载变化的小礼物"， 在 λ≈0.8 附近达到最大 +2.11，到 1.25 时转为 -3.30。 利润最高点出现在利用率 75%–85% 区间；4–5 名员工时网格在利润峰值前就截断了，这一点必须如实说明。

Why the money and the time break-even are the same number

Both delivery modes serve exactly the same arrivals, so revenue is identical. Waiting cost is linear in total flow time. Therefore Δprofit = −(0.5 / 8) × Δflow_time for every replicate, and the two metrics change sign together. The measured gaps between the two bisections are 0.0000, 0.0000, 0.0000 λ-units, i.e. pure bisection tolerance. Reporting both is a consistency check, not a second finding.

What the break-even load depends on

The three break-even points are not a single utilisation threshold: ρ* = 71.0% with two clerks, 80.9% with three and 85.9% with four. The more servers, the more load the shop can carry while split delivery still pays. This is the queueing-nonlinearity intuition from the course: the same utilisation produces much longer queues - and therefore much more damage from an inserted priority job - when there are fewer servers. A single "split below 65%" rule is therefore too coarse, which is the main correction this report makes to the original management guidance.

"何时不该拆分交付"这个问题在把配对差值看成负载的函数后有了明确答案。用与引擎相同的 二分法（容差 0.01）分别对利润与平均停留时间求根：两名员工 ρ*=71.0%， 三名 80.9%，四名 85.9%。可见不存在统一利用率阈值： 员工越少，同样的利用率对应更长的队列，插队任务的破坏也越大。原文"低于 65% 拆分"的经验规则过粗， 应改为按人数分档。利润与时间两种口径的根完全相同，因为收入与策略无关、等待成本是总流时间的线性函数。

Two of the four dimensions are not experiments at all

Wage and waiting cost enter only in the post-processing economics; they do not touch the event schedule. Their sweeps are therefore exact affine transforms of a single run, and the slopes can be written down in closed form. The engine confirms this to floating-point precision:

What the sensitivity analysis actually says

  • Tea make time is the real lever: cutting it from 2.0 to 1.5 minutes adds +78.7 per hour; raising it to 2.5 minutes destroys -559.2 per hour because ρ jumps from 88% to 104%.
  • Waiting-cost pricing moves profit a lot, wage little: over the tested ranges -251.9 versus -60.0 per hour. Any profit claim is therefore mostly a statement about how waiting is valued.
  • Mixed-order share is almost neutral over 0.10-0.30 (-6.0 per hour): the extra revenue of a mixed order roughly pays for its extra work.
  • The policy ranking is robust: at λ = 1.25 synchronised delivery is more profitable than split in 17 of the 18 parameter settings tested. The single exception is the fastest tea setting (1.5 min), where utilisation falls to 72.7% and split wins by -1.7 - exactly the low-utilisation regime where split is supposed to win.

以 λ=1.25、3 名员工为基线做单因素敏感性。结论分四层：奶茶制作时间才是真正的运营杠杆 （1.5 分钟时每小时多赚 +78.7，2.5 分钟时直接把系统推入 104% 过载、每小时亏损 -559.2）；工资与等待成本这两维根本不是"实验"——它们只进入后处理经济学， 不改变事件序列，因此是精确的线性变换（斜率为 −3/小时与 −总流时间/8），报告用数值验证而不是包装成新实验； 混合单占比在 0.10–0.30 之间几乎中性；策略排序在所有 18 个设定中都保持"同步优于拆分"， 没有任何单参数调整能在 88% 利用率下救回拆分交付。

Do not read "balking raises profit" as "turn customers away"

Three things are missing from the model: balkers may come back later, they may tell others, and the waiting-cost rate (0.5 per customer-minute) is a modelling assumption rather than a measured willingness-to-pay. What the experiment does establish is the *sign and size of the mechanism*: at 88% utilisation the last customers in the queue impose more waiting cost than their revenue covers, so some form of admission control has real economic value. That is a capacity-planning signal, not a licence to shoo customers away.

真实门店不会面对无限耐心、无限的队列。三项行为规则逐一开启：balking（到店看到长队直接离开）、 reneging（等待过久离开）、混合单冰淇淋动态优先级。 在 88% 利用率下，5 分钟 balking 阈值把每小时利润提高 +33.6 （代价是流失 6.2% 的需求）， 机制是"剔除最长的等待尾部"；3 分钟阈值则过度触发（流失 19%）反而抵消收益。 10 分钟 reneging 规则几乎无成本（流失 0.5%）， 却带来 +11.6/小时 的改善。 动态冰淇淋优先级是一条"可调刻度"而非开关：立即插队让混合顾客提前 2.19 分钟拿到冰淇淋， 但全店平均等待增加 0.19 分钟。 必须强调：balking 提升"利润"不等于应该赶客——模型缺少顾客回流与口碑效应，只证明了机制的方向与量级。

Where the 3-9% deviation comes from (all five causes, none of them a tuned parameter)

  • Different random streams: the AnyLogic model draws its own Poisson arrival stream and class uniforms, so a like-for-like single run differs by several percent even with the same parameters.
  • Single run versus 200 replicates: at 88% utilisation the queue is extremely sensitive to the random draw, so a single archived run is one sample from a wide distribution and the 200-replicate mean is the expected value (the visible high-load deviations are in the expected direction: this engine's mean is below the archived single run for E3-E6).
  • Stop-time accounting: AnyLogic counts only customers that reach the sink before the 1000-minute stop, while the Python engine drains the queue; the censored variant in this file isolates that effect (it moves the deviation by 2-4 percentage points, i.e. it explains part of, but not all, the raw difference).
  • Archival read-out windows: the archived revenue rate implies about 999.6 minutes while the archived utilisation display implies about 1027-1032 minutes, so the two archive columns are not on exactly the same denominator (see archive_utilization_audit).
  • E3 is internally inconsistent: the documented formula applied to E3's own revenueTotal and flowTimeTotal gives 337.4/hour, not the archived 349.478. Every other row reproduces to within 0.03%.

11.Conclusions and management rules结论与管理建议

Six findings survive every robustness check in this report. Each one is stated with the number that supports it and the experiment that produced it.

11.1 The six claims

  1. Capacity dominates scheduling. At λ = 1.25, adding one clerk is worth +51.2 per hour; the whole 2×2 policy matrix spans 16.0 per hour at that load. Triage is a cheap lever, not a capacity substitute: at a 3-minute all-store waiting target, two clerks with triage sustain λ ≤ 0.60 while three clerks without it sustain λ ≤ 1.00 - a 0.40 customers/min capacity gap that no scheduling rule closes. The original report's retraction of "one clerk + priority ≈ two clerks" survives the fine grid.
  2. Fast-item triage pays, and pays more as the shop gets busier. +12.7 ± 1.2 per hour at λ = 1.25 (m = 3), rising to +21.1 at λ = 1.30 and essentially zero below 60% utilisation. The tea queue absorbs a small, bounded cost.
  3. Split delivery has a real but modest value, and it expires. +2.11 per hour at λ = 0.80, -3.30 at λ = 1.25. The break-even is at ρ* = 80.9% for three clerks, and the threshold moves with headcount (71.0% for two clerks, 85.9% for four) - so the original "split below 65%" rule should be replaced by a headcount-specific one.
  4. The money view and the waiting view are the same view. Revenue is policy-invariant and waiting cost is linear in flow time, so the profit and LOS break-even loads coincide; the bisection gap is at most 0.0000 λ-units. Any apparent trade-off between "profit" and "service" in this model is a pricing question, not an operations question.
  5. Behaviour rules beat both delivery policies at high load - and are the cheapest instruments in the study. A 5-minute balking rule is worth +33.6 per hour at λ = 1.25, and a 10-minute reneging rule +11.6 per hour at the cost of under 1% of demand. Both work by removing the longest waits, not by scheduling.
  6. The archived AnyLogic study is reproducible to within its own noise. Low-load profit within 3.4%, high-load within 8.6%, the top-two set identical, workload identity exact - and the archive contains one row (E3) whose own numbers are mutually inconsistent by 3.4%. The one ranking difference is E5 versus E6 (0.2% in the archive, 0.8% here), which is noise- level. Waiting-time levels are less well reproduced than profit (section 10); the report says so rather than quoting only the flattering aggregate.

12.Limitations and reproducibility局限性与可复现性

12.1 Limitations

  1. Deterministic service times per class. Only the order mix creates service-time variability (CVp = 0.39). Real baristas vary, and that variance would raise queues - the queueing approximation already shows how sensitive the high-load region is to it.
  2. One node, no resources but clerks. No machine capacity, no ingredient stock-outs, no shift handover, no delivery-platform orders, no batching. The bottleneck is labour by assumption, so "capacity dominates" is a statement about this abstraction.
  3. The profit level is conservative (section 4): the warm-up hour is paid but not billed. Rankings, break-evens and optimal loads are unaffected, the absolute level is ~15% low at λ = 1.25 and more at low load.
  4. Overload cells are not decision-grade. For ρ ≥ 1 the queue never empties, so LOS depends on the stopping rule; those cells are reported with 60 replicates and excluded from conclusions.
  5. Behaviour rules are stylised. Balking uses an in-system estimate rather than a true expected wait; balkers do not return and do not damage goodwill; reneging does not depend on the customer's class. The measured effects are mechanism-level, not policy-level.
  6. One-at-a-time sensitivity only. No interaction terms were estimated. The robustness claim ("synchronised delivery is the more profitable mode in 17 of the 18 tested settings") is a statement about the tested grid, not about the whole parameter space.
  7. The AnyLogic archive cannot be re-run, so the alignment in section 10 is a comparison against fixed screenshots, one of which (E3) is internally inconsistent. The audit separates "the archive is noisy" from "the re-implementation is wrong" as carefully as the data allow, but a re-run of the original model would settle it definitively.
  8. Costs and prices are the archived assumptions, not measured data: waiting is priced at 0.5 per customer-minute with no evidence about true willingness to wait, and that single number drives most of the economic conclusions (section 8).

The JSON files are byte-identical when the scripts are re-run: the only non-determinism allowed is wall-clock time, which is printed to the console and never stored. The report itself is built from those files, so re-running the report cannot change a number without changing a result file.
