Intra-day demand curve: same daily mean, different peaks 日内需求曲线滑块：日均客流不变，只改变峰值形状

Drag the slider to make the day peakier. The daily mean arrival rate is held fixed: every point models the same total demand, only its shape changes. Watch the tail (p95) and the walk-away rate deteriorate while the day-average utilisation barely moves — the same trap the demand study reports at 100 replicates.

One traced day一天的事件级排队时间轴（预计算 trace，浏览器只回放）

The engine records every arrival, start and done event. This panel replays one traced replicate: the top band is the number of customers waiting, the swim-lanes show which station each order occupied, and the counters track the day as it unfolds. Nothing is simulated in the browser — the trace is precomputed.

t = 0 min (09:00)
